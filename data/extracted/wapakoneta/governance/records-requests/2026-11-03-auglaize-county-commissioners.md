## Why this request exists

The register's §1.8 (`data/extracted/wapakoneta/data-centers.md`) asks who funds WAEDC. The
County's posted minutes for 2023–2026 show two reimbursements to WAEDC, with no amounts, and no
appropriation. They also show the County Administrator reporting on "the monthly WAEDC meeting".
The minutes list resolutions by title only, so an appropriation inside the annual appropriation
resolution, or a payment by warrant, would not show there. The Journal and the Auditor's records
would.

## Scoping notes

The request is narrow on purpose, like the City request
(`2026-10-18-wapakoneta-clerk-of-council.md`). Each item names a counterparty and a date range so
it can be answered on its own. It asks for payments and agreements, not for WAEDC's own records:
whether WAEDC is a public office for R.C. 149.43 purposes is `[open]` and would be its own request.

## Draft text

> **Public Records Request — R.C. 149.43**
>
> To the Clerk of the Board, Auglaize County Board of Commissioners (copy: County Auditor):
>
> I am an individual requester. Under R.C. 149.43(B) I request copies of the following public
> records. I ask that they be provided electronically (PDF) at no charge where they exist in
> electronic form, per R.C. 149.43(B)(6)–(7).
>
> 1. **Records of payments by Auglaize County to the Wapakoneta Area Economic Development
>    Council** from 2022-01-01 to the date of this request, such as warrant or check register
>    entries or a vendor history report, showing each payment's date, amount and fund. This
>    includes the reimbursements the Board approved on or about 2024-04-25 and 2026-04-02.
>
> 2. **Any agreement, resolution or appropriation line** under which the County funds, contracts
>    with or is a member of the Wapakoneta Area Economic Development Council, in force at any time
>    since 2022-01-01.
>
> 3. **Any record appointing or designating a County official** (including the County
>    Administrator or a Commissioner) **to the board of the Wapakoneta Area Economic Development
>    Council**, or as the County's representative to it, since 2016-01-01.
>
> If any responsive record is withheld in whole or in part, please identify the record and cite
> the specific exemption relied upon, as R.C. 149.43(B)(3) requires, and release any segregable
> portion. If any item above is ambiguous or overly broad, please contact me and give me the
> opportunity to revise it before denying it, as R.C. 149.43(B)(2) contemplates. Please also tell
> me how your office maintains these records, so that I can revise the request accordingly.
>
> Please acknowledge receipt. I am happy to receive these on a rolling basis as they become
> available rather than waiting for a complete response.
>
> Thank you for your time.
>
> Cory Parent

### Constructive-denial watch

Ohio sets no fixed statutory deadline. R.C. 149.43(B)(1) requires production "within a reasonable
period of time". An unreasonable delay is treated as a **constructive denial**, which is what
makes a mandamus action under R.C. 149.43(C) available. See `data/extracted/legal/prr-mandamus/`
for the worked example.

## What each item closes in the register

- Item 1 puts a paid County figure, or a paid zero, on §1.8.
- Item 2 settles whether the County funds WAEDC beyond ad hoc reimbursements.

  Administrator's attendance at WAEDC meetings is a board seat, which stays `[open]`.
