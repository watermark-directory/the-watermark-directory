## Why this request exists

The register (`data/extracted/wapakoneta/data-centers.md`) has gaps that the open web cannot
fill, because the records exist only at the City:

- **The AEP feasibility study.** The City's own statement says it "requested an initial
  feasibility study with American Electric Power (AEP)" and that the results "indicated that the
  facility could be served without improvements to AEP's existing infrastructure". It is the one
  technical document behind the 50 MW figure, and it is not public (§1.1, §1.6).
- **The 2024 ordinances as signed.** Ord. 2024-46 and 2024-48 are read only in American Legal's
  codification, which disclaims itself (§2).
- **Ord. 2026-20 and the Regensyn agreement.** The minutes record an ordinance authorizing "a
  cooperation agreement". The agreement is the instrument, and its subject is disputed between the
  minutes ("forever chemicals") and the newspaper (TDS) (§2a).
- **The zoning of WCOIC.** If the parcels are not M-3, a data center there needs a rezoning before
  any conditional use (§2).

## Scoping notes (why the request is written the way it is)

The request is narrow on purpose, as in the Findlay request
(`data/extracted/findlay/governance/records-requests/2026-07-31-findlay-clerk-of-council.md`).
Each item names an instrument, a counterparty and a date range, so each can be answered on its
own and a partial response is still useful. Item 5 is the one correspondence item, and it is
bounded by counterparty, subject and a window that starts 2026-01-01, which is the WDN story's
"about 9 months ago" first contact, rounded back.

**What is deliberately left out:**

- **Executive-session content (2026-06-15, 2026-09-08).** Ohio's Open Meetings Act does not
  require minutes of an executive session beyond the motion, and what was discussed would be
  withheld anyway. Item 5's correspondence is the lawful route to the same question.
- **WAEDC's own records.** The City calls WAEDC "a separate entity from the city". Whether it is a
  public office for R.C. 149.43 purposes (the functional-equivalency test) is `[open]` and would
  be its own request. The City's correspondence *with* WAEDC is in item 5.
- **Anything exempt on its face.** No personnel, attorney-client or security records are asked
  for. The AEP study may be claimed as a trade secret, or as critical-infrastructure information.
  R.C. 149.43(B)(3) then requires the City to name the exemption, and that answer is worth logging
  in its own right.

> **Public Records Request — R.C. 149.43**
>
> To the Clerk of Council, City of Wapakoneta (copy: Safety Service Director):
>
> I am an individual requester. Under R.C. 149.43(B) I request copies of the following public
> records. I ask that they be provided electronically (PDF) at no charge where they exist in
> electronic form, per R.C. 149.43(B)(6)–(7).
>
> 1. **The feasibility study the City requested from American Electric Power (AEP)** concerning
>    electric service to a proposed data center at the West Central Ohio Industrial Center, as
>    described in the City Administration's public statement of October 2026. Please include the
>    City's written request to AEP, AEP's written response or report, and any attachments.
>
> 2. **Ordinance 2024-46** (amending Section 1250.10(174), the zoning definition of "warehouse")
>    and **Ordinance 2024-48** (amending Section 1282.04, the M-3 conditional permitted uses), each
>    as enacted on 2024-11-18 and signed, with the Clerk's certification.
>
> 3. **Ordinance 2026-20** (authorizing a cooperation agreement with Regensyn Energy LLC), as
>    enacted on 2026-04-20 and signed, **and the cooperation agreement it authorized**, as
>    executed, with all exhibits and attachments.
>
> 4. **The official zoning map**, or the sheet of it, showing the zoning district of the parcels
>    that make up the West Central Ohio Industrial Center, and any ordinance since 2024-01-01
>    rezoning any of those parcels.
>
> 5. **Correspondence, including e-mail,** dated **2026-01-01 to the date of this request**
>    between the Mayor, the Safety Service Director, the Law Director or the Utilities
>    Department, and any of: **American Electric Power; American Municipal Power, Inc.; the
>    Wapakoneta Area Economic Development Council; or Colocation Technology Services LLC** — in
>    each case concerning a proposed data center or data-processing facility.
>
> 6. **Any application** for a conditional use permit, zoning certificate or rezoning for a
>    "data warehouse (center)" filed with the City or its Planning Commission since 2024-11-18. If
>    there is none, I would be grateful for a statement saying so.
>
> If any responsive record is withheld in whole or in part, please identify the record and cite
> the specific exemption relied upon, as R.C. 149.43(B)(3) requires, and release any segregable
> portion. If any item above is ambiguous or overly broad, please contact me and give me the
> opportunity to revise it before denying it, as R.C. 149.43(B)(2) contemplates. Please also tell
> me how your office maintains these records, so that I can revise the request accordingly.
>
> Please acknowledge receipt. I am happy to receive these on a rolling basis as they become
> available rather than waiting for a complete response.
>
> Thank you for your time.
>
> Cory Parent

### Constructive-denial watch

Ohio sets no fixed statutory deadline. R.C. 149.43(B)(1) requires production "within a reasonable
period of time". An unreasonable delay is treated as a **constructive denial**, which is what
makes a mandamus action under R.C. 149.43(C) available. See `data/extracted/legal/prr-mandamus/`
for the worked example.
