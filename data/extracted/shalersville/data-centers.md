- **2026-10-06:** trustees amended the zoning resolution to place loads of **25 MW or more** in
  the heavy-industrial district, a district in which the township reportedly has no land
  `[reference]`. The resolution number and text are `[open]`.
- **Moratorium:** reported to lapse in early November 2026 `[reference]`. The original
  moratorium resolution and its end date are `[open]`.
- `[inference]` If both reports hold, a 25 MW+ project has no zoned district to locate in once
  the moratorium lapses, unless land is rezoned to heavy industrial. That would make the next
  rezoning application, not the moratorium, the decision point. Confirm against the adopted text.
