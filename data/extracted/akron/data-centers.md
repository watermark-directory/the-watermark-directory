# Akron / Summit Co., OH — Data-Center Activity Register

Discover-and-pin register for the Akron watershed point (upper **Cuyahoga** / Cuyahoga Valley
National Park). Status **as of 2026-11-02** (#1290). Tags are BOSC evidentiary discipline:
`[verified]` = cited primary/government source, `[reference]` = secondary source (leads only, never
a finding), `[inference]` = arithmetic or pattern from cited inputs, `[open]` = not found in any
public source yet. **Nothing here is in the BOSC corpus yet.** This records the public record and
the primary instruments to *pull*, cited by URL. Every figure is cited or marked `[open]`. Do not
bridge the Lima/Allen County graph onto Summit County; there is no evidentiary link.

**The finding is a rule, not a project.** Akron regulated the use: a conditional-use regime, not a
moratorium. The one operator on record (Ark) is an expansion supported by a state tax credit.

## Disambiguation guardrail — run first, always

- **The City of Akron, Summit County, Ohio** is the scope. Ark's **Independence** site is in
  Cuyahoga County, not Summit, and is not an Akron entry.
- **Akron, Indiana is not Akron, Ohio.** An abc57 (South Bend, IN) story, "Developer unveils data
  center plan in Akron amid resident concerns", is by its outlet almost certainly about Akron,
  Fulton County, Indiana `[inference]`. It is excluded until its county is confirmed `[open]`.
- **O-251-2026 is not a moratorium.** Do not count Akron among moratorium jurisdictions.

## 1 — Ordinance O-251-2026: data centers as a conditional use

- `[verified]` "Summary of Ordinance No. 251-2026 passed by Akron City Council July 27, 2026",
  signed by Sara Biviano, Clerk of Council. It amends Zoning Code §§ **153.140** (definitions),
  **153.464** and **153.476** "to define and specify conditional use development requirements for
  data centers; and declaring an emergency."
- Press and the sweep report add that the developer pays for the City's studies and consultants,
  and that the Planning Commission voted 5–0 `[reference]`. Neither is quoted from the summary;
  read the full ordinance before relying on them.
- **Effective date:** passed with an emergency clause `[verified]`. The effective date itself is
  `[open]`.
