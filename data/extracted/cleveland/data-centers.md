## 1 — Lakeland Equity Group, 3560 E. 55th St (rejected)

- **Project:** Lakeland Equity Group, about **$1.6 billion**, **150 MW**, on about **35 acres** at
  3560 E. 55th St (the Morabito site, near Slavic Village) `[reference]` (Data Center Dynamics;
  Cleveland Magazine).
- **Status:** the City rejected the permit on **2026-05-14** (Mayor Bibb) `[reference]`. The
  rejection letter or permit decision is `[open]`.
- **Water draw:** `[open]`.

## 2 — The moratorium: Ord. No. 556-2026 and its extension

- `[verified]` **Ordinance No. 556-2026**, an emergency ordinance, **passed 2026-07-15**,
  **effective 2026-07-20**. Sponsors: Slife, Kazy, Santana, Griffin. Source: the City's Legistar
  record.
- `[verified]` The City's own news release says Council approved a temporary, about **3-month**
  pause on new standalone data centers, by a **14–1** vote.
- `[verified]` **Extended 2026-10-19 through 2027-01-29.** Council's 2026-10-19 meeting highlights
  say Council "passed Ordinance 1246-2023 exercising Council's prerogative under Ordinance No.
  556-2026 … through January 29, 2027." Press adds that the extension also covers expansions and
  runs until permanent rules pass, if sooner `[reference]` (GovTech).
- `[open]` **The extension's ordinance number.** "1246-2023" is as printed, and a 2023 number
  cannot extend a 2026 ordinance, so it is very likely a typo for 1246-2026 `[inference]`. Confirm
  it in Legistar before citing.
