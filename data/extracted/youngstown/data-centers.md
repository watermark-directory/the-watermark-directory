# Youngstown / Mahoning Co., OH — Data-Center Activity Register

Discover-and-pin register for the Youngstown watershed point (**Mahoning** River mainstem). Status
**as of 2026-11-02** (#1292). Tags are BOSC evidentiary discipline: `[verified]` = cited
primary/government source, `[reference]` = secondary source (leads only, never a finding),
`[inference]` = arithmetic or pattern from cited inputs, `[open]` = not found in any public source
yet. **Nothing here is in the BOSC corpus yet.** This records the public record and the primary
instruments to *pull*, cited by URL. Every figure is cited or marked `[open]`. Do not bridge the
Lima/Allen County graph onto Mahoning County; there is no evidentiary link.

**The finding is a pursuit with no project.** The record shows City leaders seeking a data center,
not a named developer, parcel or instrument. Youngstown carries no facility.

## 3 — Context: Hubbard, Trumbull County ("Project Milo")

- **Project:** "Project Milo", an unnamed operator brought through JobsOhio and Lake to River, on a
  site discussed as near the former Deer Creek Golf Course `[reference]`. A tracker attributes it
  to **Crane Data Centers**; that is unconfirmed `[open]`.
- **NDA:** the mayor signed a nondisclosure agreement in 2024 `[reference]`. The NDA's counterparty
  is `[open]`.
- **Moratorium:** Council passed a **12-month** data-center moratorium in June 2026 and formed a
  committee in July `[reference]` (Tribune Chronicle). The ordinance number is `[open]`.
- **Ballot:** a citizen-initiative ordinance **banning data centers inside city limits** is on the
  **2026-11-03** ballot `[reference]` (WKBN). States Newsroom calls it the only full-ban city vote
  in Ohio `[reference]`. The ballot language from the Trumbull County Board of Elections is
  `[open]`, and the result post-dates this register.
- **Basin:** `[open]`. Do not assume Hubbard drains to the Mahoning.
