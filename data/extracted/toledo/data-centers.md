## 1 — Oregon: Vantage via Capacity, Wynn & Corduroy roads

### 1.1 — What the press reported `[reference]`

- City officials named **Vantage Data Centers** as the prospective developer near **Wynn and
  Corduroy roads** (13abc 2026-07-29). **Will Turner** of Capacity Infrastructure LLC is described
  as site selector and intermediary, with Vantage as end user.
- Council approved a **2024 purchase agreement** with Capacity LLC to sell about **170 acres** for
  **$17.3M** (13abc).
- On-site power: three clusters of 12 natural-gas generators, eventually **828 MW**, per Capacity's
  website as reported (13abc). A developer's self-description — not a permit.
- An economic-development agreement and a permanent site plan must still be approved by Council
  (13abc). Their status as of this date is `[open]`.

### 1.2 — Regulatory record

- No OEPA air PTI and no OPSB filing for the 828 MW of generation has been found `[open]`.

## 2 — Oregon charter amendment: data centers over 25 MW (Nov 3, 2026 ballot)

- `[reference]` The Lucas County BOE certified the signatures and Council voted unanimously to accept
  the certification; the measure is on the November ballot (13abc 2026-08-19). The network ledger
  (`data/extracted/legal/datacenter-legislation/local-ballot-2026-11.md`) still lists Oregon as
  **unconfirmed**; this register does not change that row. The BOE record is `[open]`, and a ballot
  measure is not an outcome.
