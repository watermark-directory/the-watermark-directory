## 1 — Bristolville 25 Developer, L.L.C. / BHGH Properties parcel

- **Developer and parcel:** Bristolville 25 Developer, L.L.C. agreed in February 2025 to buy from
  BHGH Properties, L.L.C. a Lordstown parcel "zoned for industrial use", on which it seeks to build
  a data center `[verified]`. Source: *State ex rel. Bristolville 25 Developer, L.L.C. v.
  Lordstown*, Slip Opinion No. 2026-Ohio-4073, ¶ 3.
- **Size and cost:** four three-story buildings on about **133 acres**, about **$3.6 billion**

- **MW, water draw, operator/tenant:** `[open]`.

### 1.1 — The Village's instruments, as the Supreme Court recites them

All `[verified]` from the opinion (2026-Ohio-4073, ¶¶ 11–17), which takes judicial notice of the
Village's ordinances and cites each one's PDF on lordstown.com:

- **2025-10-20:** Bristolville hand-delivered step-one site-plan materials. The next day the
  planning-and-zoning administrator replied that the office "would not be able to proceed" until
  Council decided on a recommended data-center ban, then at second reading.
- **2025-11-03:** Council passed **Ord. No. 57-2025** as an emergency measure, prohibiting any data
  center within the Village. It was to take effect 2025-12-03 and exempted facilities existing or
  under construction.
- **Before 2025-12-03:** Council **repealed** 57-2025 before it took effect and unanimously passed
  **Ord. No. 1-2026**, a temporary moratorium on issuing new permits and on receiving and processing
  permit applications for data centers. It ran up to 180 days, to expire 2026-07-04.
- **2026-06-15:** Council passed **Ord. No. 29-2026**, extending the moratorium for another 180
  days.
- `[inference]` 180 days from 2026-07-04 is 2026-12-31. The opinion does not state the extended
  expiry, so read 29-2026 before quoting a date.

### 1.2 — The mandamus case (decided 2026-10-20)

- `[verified]` *State ex rel. Bristolville 25 Developer, L.L.C. v. Lordstown*, No. 2025-1497,
  submitted 2026-08-04, **decided 2026-10-20**, 2026-Ohio-4073. It is a per curiam opinion joined
  by all seven justices. **Writ denied.**
- `[verified]` The holding (¶ 48): "Under the moratorium, the village is under no legal duty to
  proceed with a site-plan review of Bristolville's project under Lordstown Cod.Ord. 1115.04 and
  relators have not shown a clear legal right to have Bristolville's site plan reviewed under the
  premoratorium zoning ordinance." The Court did not reach the Village's adequate-remedy argument.
- `[verified]` On vested rights (¶ 46), the Court distinguished *Gibson v. Oberlin* and *Fairmount
  Ctr. Co. v. Arnold*: those owners had met the legislative requirements for a permit, and
  Bristolville had not. That is the reason the claimed vested right fails. It is not a separate
  holding about "prerequisites", which is how some press framed the decision.
- **Consequence:** while the moratorium stands, the site plan does not move. The developer's route
  after it lapses is `[open]`.
