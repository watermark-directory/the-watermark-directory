## 1 — Aligned Data Centers NEO-01, Perkins Township

### 1.1 — What the press reported `[reference]`

- **129 acres at 2509 Hayes Ave, Perkins Twp** — the former KBI plant, bought for about **$52M**;
  groundbreaking **May 2024**; four buildings, the first about **96 MW / 200k sq ft** (DCD).
- **Perkins Twp / Perkins Local Schools economic-development agreement:** 30% of new taxes to the
  township and schools, 70% to the project, phase 1 (Perkins Schools board notice). Whether this is a
  TIF, and its instrument number, is `[open]`.

  filing for it has been found `[open]`.
