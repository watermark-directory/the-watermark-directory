Discover-and-pin register for the Lancaster watershed point (upper Hocking River). Status **as of
2026-11-02** (#1293). Tags are BOSC evidentiary discipline: `[verified]` = cited primary/government
source, `[reference]` = secondary source or a party's own self-report (leads only, never a
finding), `[inference]` = arithmetic or pattern from cited inputs, `[open]` = not found in any

not.

## Disambiguation guardrail — run first, always

- **Fairfield County, Ohio (FIPS 39045).** Not Lancaster County, Pennsylvania, or Lancaster, NY.
- **Greenfield Township is Fairfield County's.** Other Ohio counties have one.
- **Millersport is in Fairfield County but on a different watershed point.** The Vantage campus and
  OPSB 26-160-EL-BGN (§2) sit at Buckeye Lake's west end, not on the Hocking at Lancaster. They are
  recorded here because no registered site is closer; they are **context, not a Lancaster entry**.
  The Columbus register already excludes "Vantage OH2 Millersport" as a Fairfield County trap.
