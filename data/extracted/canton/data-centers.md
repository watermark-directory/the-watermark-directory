# Canton / Stark Co., OH — Data-Center Activity Register

Discover-and-pin register for the Canton watershed point (Nimishillen Creek → **Tuscarawas** →
Muskingum). Status **as of 2026-11-02** (#2210). Tags are BOSC evidentiary discipline:
`[verified]` = cited primary/government source, `[reference]` = secondary source (leads only, never
a finding), `[inference]` = arithmetic or pattern from cited inputs, `[open]` = not found in any
public source yet. **Nothing here is in the BOSC corpus yet.** This records the public record and
the primary instruments to *pull*, cited by URL. Every figure is cited or marked `[open]`. Do not
bridge the Lima/Allen County graph onto Stark County; there is no evidentiary link.

**The finding is three proposals and one ordinance, all from press.** The 2026-11-02 sweep read no
Stark County primary record: the Auditor's real-estate search was down for maintenance. Every
project figure below is `[reference]` until a deed, ordinance or abatement is pulled.

## Disambiguation guardrail — run first, always

- **Canton (city) and Perry Township, Stark County, Ohio** are the scope. Massillon is also in
  Stark County. Its tracker listings (Bitdeer Massillon, Harsh Avenue) are context and are not
  entered here.
- **Perry Township is not unique.** There are Perry Townships in Allen County (the Lima register's
  Breese Road site, which Microsoft dropped on 2024-10-16) and in other Ohio counties. A press item
  says Microsoft dropped a separate ~350-acre Perry Twp proposal, but which county it was in is
  `[open]`. Do not attribute any Microsoft Perry Twp item to Stark County until the county is
  confirmed.
- **The Midwest Digital Gateway developer is reported two ways**, as Corscale and as DAMAC Digital
  `[reference]`. This register says "Midwest Digital Gateway" and leaves the developer `[open]`.

## 1 — Midwest Digital Gateway (southeast Canton)

- **Site:** about **162 acres** (163.5 in one source) southeast of Millerton St & Central Ave SE,
  annexed into the City from Canton Twp in 2025 `[reference]` (Canton Repository via Yahoo;
  Construction Review Online).
- **Scale:** **312 MW**, about **$2.9 billion** `[reference]`.
- **Abatement:** a 75% property-tax abatement is sought `[reference]`. Its instrument (CRA/EZ
  agreement, council action) is `[open]`.
- **Land:** press reports a **$72.9 million** sale to **MDG-Canton Purchaser LLC** (also printed
  "MDG Canton Purchaser LLC") `[open]`. **Unverified.** On 2026-11-02 the Stark County Auditor's
  real-estate search returned "currently unavailable due to maintenance", so no deed, parcel
  number, grantor or price was confirmed.
- **Tenant:** AWS is reported as a prospective tenant `[open]`.

## 3 — Panattoni, Perry Township (Faircrest St SW)

- About **100–105 acres**, **240 MW** `[reference]` (Baxtel; CleanView).
- Township trustees endorsed a 75% abatement on March 24 `[reference]`; the year is `[open]`. It
