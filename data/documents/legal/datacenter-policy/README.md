# legal/datacenter-policy — third-party policy research on Ohio data centers

Commissioned economic, fiscal and community-benefit studies of Ohio's data-center industry.
These are **not public records**: a third party wrote and published them. They are held so the
platform can read and cite what they report. They sit next to
[`datacenter-legislation/`](../datacenter-legislation/README.md), which holds the bills these
studies discuss, and [`select-committee-2026/`](../select-committee-2026/), the hearing record
where the same questions came up.

## Standing

- **Every claim from this shelf is `[reference]`, never `[verified]`.** When a commissioned study
  reports a figure, it is reporting someone else's record, and that record (an Ohio Department of
  Taxation actual, a TIRC filing, a PUCO order) is what a `[verified]` claim must cite.
- **Not cleared for the public site.** `legal/datacenter-policy` is not inside any cleared boundary
  in `data/site/published-documents.yaml`. Its vault entries are recorded
  `redistributable: false`, following the `wapakoneta/reference` precedent (#2184).
- **The Technical Appendix is not held.** The report's appendices (A–I) are a separate volume,
  "not published with this report" and available on request from the Foundation. Any method that
  rests on them is `[open]`.
