CHAPTER 1282 M-3 Industrial District

1282.01 INTENT.
   The M-3 District is primarily reserved for intensive industrial uses that, because of their general nature, typically produce off-site impacts such as vibration, noise, odor or other impacts that may be objectionable or potentially hazardous to surrounding properties.
(Ord. 2010-29. Passed 7-5-10.)
